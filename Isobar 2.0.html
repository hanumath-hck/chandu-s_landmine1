    Before the rains

    • Clean the roof and gutters before the first heavy rain.
    • Fit a mesh screen and a first-flush diverter so the dirtiest water is thrown away.
    • Cover the tank and seal vents with fine mesh to keep mosquitoes out.
    • Send overflow to a recharge pit or borewell so surplus rain refills groundwater.
    • Filter and boil harvested water before drinking, or use it for washing, toilets and plants.

    Collected water = rainfall (mm) × roof area (m²) × runoff factor × 0.9 for first flush and filter loss. One millimetre of rain on one square metre gives one litre. Rainfall is the last 12 full months from the Open-Meteo archive. The tank starts empty and household use draws it down each day. Results are estimates, not a design.
